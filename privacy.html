Gym Receipts

Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-04

Support · Terms · Privacy

Gym Receipts is a gym check-in app: you take a live proof photo when you go, and your friends and groups see it. This page says what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. In short: no ads, no tracking, no contacts, no location, and your photos go only to people you chose.

What we don't collect

Email address (Sign in with Apple is set to share no email and no name with us), phone number, contacts, location, health or fitness data, advertising identifier, browsing or search history, payment details, crash reports. Your birth date at sign-up is checked on your phone (13 and over) and never stored or sent. There are no ads and no tracking, so the app never asks for tracking permission.

Who can see what

  • Your profile card (username, display name) is visible to someone who types your exact username, opens an invite link you shared, or is in a group with you; there is no browsing or suggested people.
  • Your check-ins and photos are visible only to your friends and to members of groups you share, and never to people you blocked or who blocked you. Photos are stored privately and shown through links that expire after an hour.
  • Group and challenge names and leaderboards are visible to that group's members.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase hosts the database, the photos and the server functions, as our processor.
  • TelemetryDeck receives the anonymous usage data, only while it's turned on.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers notifications.
  • Our moderators review each report: what was reported, the reason, and the reported photo.

We don't sell data and don't share it with anyone else.

On your phone

Check-ins and photos are saved on your iPhone first, so the app works offline, and are sent once you're online and signed in. Without an account nothing leaves the phone except the anonymous usage data (if it's on). Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis can blur photos from others on your phone if you turned on Sensitive Content Warning; it runs on the phone and sends nothing to us.

Keeping and deleting

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. It deletes your profile, check-ins, photos, reactions, friendships and memberships from our servers, revokes the Sign in with Apple link, and wipes the app's data on that phone.
  • Reports you filed are kept without any link to your account, so moderators keep a safety record.
  • Delete a single check-in: Stats tab > tap its day > long-press the receipt > Delete check-in. It is removed from the server too.
  • Anonymous usage data is not linked to you, so it can't be found or deleted per person.

Children

You must be 13 or older to make an account. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.
