Checking in

  • How do I check in? At the gym, open the app and tap I WENT. Take the photo with the camera (photos are always live; the photo library isn't used). One check-in a day counts; extra ones are kept as photos.
  • When does the day end? At 3:00 AM where you are, so a late-night session counts for the day it started.
  • I was offline. Check-ins save on your phone and send when you're back online (the receipt says QUEUED, WILL SYNC). A check-in for yesterday still counts if it reaches the server before 9:00 AM.
  • New phone or reinstalled? Sign in with the same Apple ID. Your check-ins come back; photos download as you view them.
  • The camera doesn't open. Settings app > Gym Receipts > Camera: on.
  • No reminders? In the app, Settings > Reminders; and in the Settings app, Notifications > Gym Receipts: allowed.

Friends, groups and challenges

  • Add a friend: Feed tab > Friends: search their exact @username, or share your invite link or QR code.
  • Join a group: open the invite link or scan its QR code, or Groups > Join with a link.
  • Challenges have no prizes or fees; they're bragging rights.

Safety

  • Report a photo, person, group or challenge from its ... menu or the flag in the photo viewer. It disappears for you at once and our moderators review it within 24 hours.
  • Block someone from their profile, a feed item or a leaderboard row. You'll stop seeing each other everywhere.
  • Something urgent or unsafe? Email us with "URGENT" in the subject. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number.

Your account and data

  • Delete your account: Settings > Account > Delete account. Everything on our servers goes, and the app's data on that phone is wiped.
  • Anonymous usage data: Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous usage data: off.
